The community advantage

More people, more possibilities

FitTogether is a local, two-sided network: members find workout partners, while gyms and trainers bring activities to the community.

Member ↔ member

Same-side network effect

Each additional person in the same area gives members more potential partners with compatible goals and schedules. A concentrated local group is more useful than the same number spread far apart.

Members ↔ organizers

Cross-side network effect

More members make FitTogether more valuable to gyms, trainers, and community hosts. Their classes and events then give members more reasons to join and return.
