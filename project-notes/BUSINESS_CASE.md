## FitTogether Business Case Report

Your fitness journey is better together



## 1. The idea FitTogether

Customer - FitTogether is intended for young adults, students, and anyone else who is looking to become more active. It also targets community organizers, fitness instructors, and gyms who wish to reach out to new members.
Problem - Many people struggle to stay motivated in the gym when working out alone or finding workout partners with similar goals and schedules. Today people rely on friends, social media or word of mouth to find good workout partners. This makes finding partners to train with hard and sometimes stressful. 
Solution - FitTogether provides a platform where users can find workout partners, join local communities, compete in challenges and track your progress simply. When looking for a partner users can filter based on fitness goals, experience, availability and location based on what they're looking for. This makes finding a compatible workout partner incredible easy and encourages motivation. 
Revenue - Although the website is free to sign up for and use FitTogether could make revenue through partnerships with local gyms and fitness trainers who will pay to advertise there services, classes and events. If later on you want to increase revenue on FitTogether you can offer a premium membership that will give users additional features like personalized challenges and advanced partner matching. 

## 2. Where IT creates value

FitTogether creates value by changing how people find workout partners, discover fitness communities, and stay motivated. The website makes connecting with people who share similar fitness goals, schedules, and experience levels much easier all in one place. Many people go to the gym alone with little knowledge of what workouts to do and the best thing for them. FitTogether brings people with trainers or users that can help at the gym. Several course concepts from class apply to fitTogether like scale, Scale is important because as more people join, there are more potential workout partners and communities available. This creates a network effect where the platform becomes more valuable as its membership grows. Distribution channels are improved because users can access FitTogether online from anywhere instead of depending on in-person connections. Differentiation comes from combining workout partner matching, fitness communities, challenges, and progress tracking into one platform. Data creates value by allowing users to filter potential partners based on their fitness goals, experience, availability, and location. Finally switching costs could develop as FitTogether creates partnerships with gyms and when users develop friendships and join communities making them not want to switch to other websites. Overall, IT allows FitTogether to connect people more efficiently while creating opportunities for long-term growth.

## 3. Network effects

FitTogether becomes more valuable as more people join because users have more opportunities to find workout partners, join fitness communities, and participate in challenges. It is a two-sided market because it connects two main groups: fitness members looking for workout partners and communities, and gyms, trainers, and fitness organizers offering classes, events, and services. As the network grows, members have more potential matches and activities to choose from. At the same time, gyms and trainers benefit from reaching a larger audience of potential customers. This creates both same-side and cross-side network effects, making the platform more useful to everyone involved. To solve the empty network problem at launch I would start with a local area I know like STFX or my hometown community and invite 30-50 people to join the website. I would also partner with a gym or maybe two local ones and get them on the website along with their trainers. This would create an active community right off of launch and make the website run with many options. My prototype demonstrates these concepts in the Network Effects section. It includes an interactive slider showing how increasing the number of local members affects potential partner matches, fitness communities, organizers, and activities. 


## 4. Can it be copied? (the moat question)

FitTogether’s competitive advantage is not the website itself because AI tools make it relatively easy for competitors to build a similar platform. Its real value comes from creating a strong local fitness community and building relationships between members, gyms, and trainers. The platform is valuable because it helps people find workout partners, stay motivated, and become more consistent with exercise. It could become rare by developing a strong network of active members in a specific area, such as STFX or halifax. It would also become harder to imitate over time because competitors cannot instantly copy established relationships, community trust, and member engagement. Once FitTogether becomes popular in one area you can then expand out to more specific communities until it becomes very popular. A competitor with a bigger budget could recreate the website and its basic features fairly quickly, possibly within a few weeks. However, building an active and successful community would take much longer. They would need to attract members, create partnerships with local gyms, promote the platform, and develop a trustworthy reputation. These things require time and consistent effort, not just money. FitTogether's strongest long-term advantages would be its network effects, member data, and brand recognition. As more people join, members have more potential workout partners, fitness groups, and activities to choose from. This makes the platform more valuable and encourages people to continue using it. Over time, FitTogether could also learn more about members' fitness goals, interests, and schedules, helping improve partner recommendations. Building strong community connections and customer loyalty would make it harder for competitors to take members away. overall, FitTogether's biggest advantage would be the community it creates, rather than the technology used to build the website which can never be replaced.
